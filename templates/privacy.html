Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information Spendly collects, why, and what control you have over it.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address.
  • Your password: stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, never as plain text.
  • Your expenses: the amounts, categories, dates and notes you enter.

2. How we use it

  • to sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • to show your expenses and the summaries built from them.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use your expenses for advertising.

3. Cookies

Spendly uses a single session cookie to keep you signed in. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

4. Who can see your data

Only you can see your expenses through the app. We only share information if the law requires us to.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. If you close your account, your details and expenses are deleted.

6. Your choices

You can view, edit or delete your expenses at any time, and update your details from your profile.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. See also our Terms and Conditions.
