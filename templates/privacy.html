Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information Spendly collects, why we need it, and what control you have over it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account details: your name and email address.
  • Your password: stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, never as plain text.
  • Your expenses: the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.

We don't ask for bank details, card numbers or access to your bank accounts. Spendly only knows what you choose to type in.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • to sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • to show your expenses, category breakdowns and monthly summaries;
  • to keep you signed in, using a single session cookie.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use your expenses for advertising or tracking.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expenses are kept in Spendly's database for as long as you have an account. Only you can see your expenses through the app. You can edit or delete any expense at any time, and if you close your account, your details and expenses are deleted.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly doesn't share your expenses with anyone. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers and shares basic technical information such as your IP address. We don't use third-party analytics or advertising services. We only share information with others if the law requires us to.

5. Contact Us

If you have a question about this policy or your data, email us at privacy@example.com. If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. See also our Terms and Conditions.
